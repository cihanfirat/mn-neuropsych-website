Our Mission

As a student-led non-profit, our mission is to support our local community in every way we can. We drive meaningful change by:

🧠

Advocating for Change

Championing our Digital Awareness Bill with Minnesota state legislators.

🤝

Serving Others

Organizing blanket drives, donation campaigns, and direct support for families in need.

🌱

Promoting Wellness & Growth

Raising mental health awareness while offering our members hands-on professional development.

Through every initiative, our team remains dedicated to making a lasting, positive impact on the world around us.

Why Start a Chapter?

Establishing a NeuroPsych chapter connects you with a passionate network of individuals dedicated to making a tangible impact. As a chapter leader, you will:

Lead Meaningful Change

Engage with state legislators, host fundraising events with our team's support, and spearhead mental health awareness campaigns.

Serve Your Community

Take direct action through local service projects and community outreach initiatives.

Build Professional Networks

Connect with healthcare professionals, policymakers, and business leaders.

Develop Real-World Skills

Gain hands-on experience in healthcare, government, and business while refining your technical and interpersonal skills within a diverse, driven team.

Where Our Funds Go

Every dollar raised is reinvested directly into our community and our mission.

  • 25% — Local Outreach

    Covers all supplies for our volunteer visits to local care facilities.

  • 25% — Family Support

    Supplies vital resources to Twin Cities families facing neurodegenerative diseases.

  • 50% — Growing Our Mission

    Goes toward shirts, banners, pamphlets, and everyday operations to keep our organization running.

Half of every dollar directly funds our community service, and half powers our operations so we can keep making an impact.
